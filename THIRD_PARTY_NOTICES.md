## Historical portraits

The three selected demo images are public-domain files from Wikimedia Commons. Original image bytes are preserved; the interface displays them in grayscale using CSS. Portrait metadata, source URLs, credited creators, licences, and SHA-256 hashes are in [attributions.json](public/example-assets/attributions.json).

Newly generated reports resolve their own portrait and attribution through the generic Wikimedia/Wikidata pipeline. Their licences may differ from these examples; preserve the returned licence and credit when sharing.

## Research and biographical records

Public examples contain publication titles and metadata with DOI/OpenAlex links, plus AI-written interpretations. They do not redistribute publication abstracts or Wikipedia biography extracts. The abstract coverage counts describe the evidence used during the original analysis, not text bundled in the demo.

[OpenAlex](https://help.openalex.org/), [Wikidata](https://www.wikidata.org/wiki/Wikidata:Licensing), and [NobelPrize.org](https://www.nobelprize.org/) remain linked as the relevant sources. This project is independent of these services and the people featured.
